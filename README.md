# Scorchit Raw: one-knob formant-linked saturator

![Scorchit Raw free one-knob formant-linked saturation UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/ScorchitRaw/main/scorchitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your signal gets hotter, with a vocal, formant-like character that opens up as it drives.**

Scorchit Raw is the free one-knob version of **Scorchit**, a formant-linked saturator for producers who just want to grab a knob and add heat. All the tone-shaping happens under the hood, so there is nothing to set up.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/ScorchitRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`ScorchitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Scorchit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`ScorchitRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Scorchit Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **SCORCH** knob. Turn it up and it drives an oversampled saturation engine whose vocal formant character opens up with the heat. Everything is auto-leveled, so pushing the knob adds harmonics and grit without volume jumps.

Under the hood it runs high-quality oversampling, a formant filter bank that glides with the drive, and a DC blocker, all driven by that single macro. No modes, no menus, no setup.
